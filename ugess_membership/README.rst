Membership subscriptions to social projects.

The basic idea of tying membership subscriptions to social projects is that
subscribers are given a budget ceiling per time unit for payments made at the
company (usually a supermarket). Furthermore, they usually receive a discount
while their social project is active. With the money saved from the discount and
the ceiling, the subscriber must achieve their social project.
