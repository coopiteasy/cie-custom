Accompagner Custom

Custom development to adapt res_partner to the context of social services for Accompagner.

Data model

On res.partner:

Partners are divided into 4 types in the field social_type:

  • accompagner, itself divided in two:
    • is_social_benefactor
    • is_social_volunteer
    • is_employee (standard field)
  • beneficiary
  • partner
  • other

Partners that have fsm_location set to True, ie. which correspond to location rather than to actual people, are filtered out from dropdowns by default. They are also hidden by default from the partners view in contacts and fieldservice -> master data -> customers.

The following fields have also been added:

  • social_beneficiary_ids / social_partner_ids: many2many relations between partners and beneficiaries (through the beneficiary_partner_rel table).
  • social_file_creation_date: date of creation of the social file (defaults to today, required).
  • Properties fields per contact type (social_beneficiary_properties, social_volunteer_properties, social_partner_properties, social_benefactor_properties), defined per company.
  • Per-type fields: orientation, GDPR, spoken language, residence permit title and residence right, contact person, civil status, children count, household type, resource and “in Belgium since” date for beneficiaries; professional status, personal email, nationality, contact method, volunteer types, motivations and volunteer course lines for volunteers; structure for partners.

On res.company:

  • Properties definitions for each social contact type (partner_properties_definition_social_beneficiary, ..._social_volunteer, ..._social_partner, ..._social_benefactor).

Configuration

The following configuration models are manageable from Contacts > Configuration > Social Service (with an “access social services configuration” group), with full access for administrators and read-only access for other internal users:

  • Orientation (partner.social.orientation)
  • Residence Permit Title (partner.social.residence.permit.title)
  • Residence Right (partner.social.residence.right)
  • Civil Status (partner.social.civil.status)
  • Household Type (partner.social.household.type)
  • Resource (partner.social.resource)
  • Volunteer Professional Status (partner.social.volunteer.professional.status)
  • Volunteer Contact Method (partner.social.volunteer.contact.method)
  • Volunteer Type (partner.social.volunteer.type)
  • Volunteer Motivation (partner.social.volunteer.motivation)
  • Structure (partner.social.structure)

Volunteer course lines (partner.social.volunteer.course) are managed directly on the partner form (editors need contact creation rights).

Security

  • An “Access Beneficiary Informations Group” group gates the visibility of the Beneficiary tab.
  • An “Access social services configuration” group gates the Social Service configuration menu.

Views adaptation

The partner form is adapted per social type:

Beneficiary (social_type == 'beneficiary'):

  • Hide the VAT and the company name field.
  • Hide the Salesperson, Purchase, Company Registry, Company and Sector of Activity fields on the Sales & Purchase tab (only the Reference and the File Creation Date stay).
  • Hide the Invoicing tab.
  • Hide the Fiscal Information group (fiscal position and tax receipt option).
  • Hide the “Tax Receipt Option” field and the “Tax Receipts” stat button (from donation_base).
  • Hide the “Partner Assignment” tab (from base_geolocalize).
  • Hide the “Field Service” tab (from fieldservice).
  • Show the “Beneficiary” tab (restricted to the “Access Beneficiary Informations Group” group) with linked partners, beneficiary properties and beneficiary information.

Accompagner (social_type == 'accompagner'):

  • Hide the VAT and the company name field.
  • Hide the Salesperson, Purchase, Company Registry, Company and Sector of Activity fields on the Sales & Purchase tab (only the Reference, the File Creation Date and the “Tax Receipt Option” stay).
  • On the Invoicing tab, keep only the Bank Accounts table (hide Customer Invoices, Accounting Entries, Credit Limits and Automation groups).
  • Hide the fiscal position field.
  • Hide the “Partner Assignment” tab (from base_geolocalize).
  • Hide the “Field Service” tab (from fieldservice).
  • Show the “Volunteer” tab (when the partner is flagged as volunteer) with volunteer properties, information and course, and the “Benefactor” tab (when the partner is flagged as benefactor) with benefactor properties.

Partner (social_type == 'partner'):

  • Hide the “Partner Assignment” tab (from base_geolocalize).
  • Hide the “Field Service” tab (from fieldservice).
  • Show the “Partner” tab with linked beneficiaries, partner properties and structure.
